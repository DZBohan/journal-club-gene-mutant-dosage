# Discussion points

- Dosage thresholds were selected to maximise survival separation and then tested on the same survival data. Would fixed thresholds replicate in an independent cohort?
- The posterior is collapsed into three classes; dosage uncertainty does not enter the risk models.
- Adjusted hazard and odds ratios are associations. Residual confounding (treatment, sampling time, patient selection) and added value over existing clinical variables remain open.
- FAM is a ratio: 1 of 2 and 4 of 8 mutant copies both give 0.5, so it is not an absolute dose.
- The model does not distinguish chromosomal amplification from extrachromosomal DNA, and total copy number is capped at 8, so it does not apply directly to highly amplified ecDNA.
