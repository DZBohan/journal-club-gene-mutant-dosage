# INCOMMON Journal Club

Journal club workspace for Calonaci et al., **Gene mutant dosage is associated with prognosis and metastatic tropism in 60,000 clinical cancer samples**, Nature Genetics (2026).

Paper: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-026-02666-z

## Format

Simulation 2: Student, PI, and Lab Consensus.

- Student presentation: 20 minutes.
- PI assessment: three specific points submitted 48 hours in advance.
- PI questioning: 10 minutes.
- Student response: 10 minutes.
- Lab discussion and vote: 10–15 minutes.

## Organization

- `presentation/`: slides and speaker notes.
- `assessment/`: assessment drafts and responses.
- `figures/`: editable teaching diagrams and exports.
- `notes/`: reading notes and references.
- `logs/`: repository work records.

This repository is initially a scaffold. Existing reading materials and diagrams have not yet been imported. Preserve original sources when importing; clearly distinguish published evidence, teaching examples, and proposed experiments.

Do not commit private correspondence, identifiable patient data, or unpublished lab results without explicit approval. Third-party materials retain their original licenses and require appropriate attribution.
