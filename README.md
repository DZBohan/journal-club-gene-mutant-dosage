## Presentation rules

White backgrounds; Arial 32 pt titles and Arial 18 pt for all other text. Configuration A/(2,1): #7ed3f6; B/(3,2): #92c86b; C/(1,1): #7fc7a5. Mutant M: #018f52; wild-type W: #2c3385. Keep editable text separate from externally drawn figures. Preserve approved layouts and teaching numbers; remove redundant exposition without removing essential methods. Do not use em dashes.

## Journal club format

Simulation 2: Student, PI, and Lab Consensus.

- Student presentation: 20 minutes.
- PI assessment: three points submitted 48 hours in advance.
- PI questioning: 10 minutes.
- Student response: 10 minutes.
- Lab discussion and vote: 10 to 15 minutes.
